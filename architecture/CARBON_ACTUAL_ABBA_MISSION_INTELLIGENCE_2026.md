# CARBON ACTUAL — ABBA MISSION INTELLIGENCE — 2026

ABBA does not move directly from an objective to execution.

The mission intelligence layer resolves:

INTENT → CONSTRAINTS → SUCCESS CRITERIA → ASSUMPTIONS → UNCERTAINTY → MISSION → DEPENDENCIES → CAPABILITY PROVENANCE → SCENARIO ANALYSIS → STEWARDSHIP → GOVERNANCE.

Specialist packs may execute in parallel where their inputs permit, then converge before consequential planning.

## Intent
Explicit human intent is distinct from inferred intent. Ambiguity is preserved and surfaced.

## Mission
Every consequential mission should identify success criteria, dependencies and required evidence. Decomposition is a proposal, not authorization.

## Uncertainty
Unknown, ambiguous, contradictory, provisional, stale and missing-evidence states are first-class. Uncertainty never becomes truth by repetition.

## Capability provenance
Capability availability, provenance, certification, permission and authority are separate. A discovered capability can be unsuitable, unavailable, unverified or unauthorized.

## Scenario analysis
Simulation explores possible outcomes and sensitivity. It is analysis only and is not evidence that a future outcome will occur.

## Stewardship
ABBA assesses value, cost, waste, risk, human impact, environmental impact, reversibility and continuity before consequential governance crossing.

No specialist pack grants authority.
