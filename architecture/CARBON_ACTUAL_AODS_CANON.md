# Carbon Actual AODS — Canonical Design & Interaction Doctrine

**Version:** 3.0.0-universal  
**Status:** Canonical technical doctrine  
**Authority:** `carbonactual/hapi-world/CANON.md`

AODS is the shared ecological visual and interaction grammar for Carbon Actual. It provides one recognizable body of work while allowing distinct product worlds.

## 435-plate coverage
Plate ordinals 1–435 are addressable. Registry addressability is distinct from historical verification. Software must not fabricate species, scientific names, habitat facts, SVG traces, or palette samples and call them authoritative.

## Eight working habitat families
1. High Aerial & Sky
2. Deep Canopy & Timber
3. Coastal Salt Lagoon
4. Wetlands & River Basins
5. Open Marine Waters
6. Forest Floor & Soil
7. Tropical & Exo-Canopy
8. Nocturnal & Ridge

## Assignment
A primary product surface declares one primary plate/habitat identity. Related submodules may use sibling ecological identities when their purpose and interaction character genuinely differ. Composite expressions are allowed when capability composition genuinely spans domains.

## Visual baseline
Light/pristine surfaces are the default where applicable. Glass layers, translucent borders, layered elevation, habitat washes, and responsive depth are reusable patterns.

The 60/30/10 chromatic split is a baseline: 60% habitat wash, 30% structural frame, 10% kinetic accent. Accessibility and semantic contrast may require deviation.

## Motion
Motion expresses habitat and operational state through reusable families such as thermal glide, raptor swoop, aquatic current, tactile spring, migration, pulse, orbit, dive, and hover.

## Identity marks
Primary marks should be custom and habitat-aware. A generic framework icon is not a brand mark. Source/provenance and licensing are recorded for traced or derived artwork.

## AI builder guardrail
AI-generated interfaces resolve capability and habitat before layout and must not silently fall back to generic framework styling when AODS applies.

## Constitutional boundary
AODS never overrides identity, authority, evidence, privacy, accessibility, security, regulated workflow, or HAPI World/Carbon Actual law.
