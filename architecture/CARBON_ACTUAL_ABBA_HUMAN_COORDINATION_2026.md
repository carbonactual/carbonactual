# CARBON ACTUAL — ABBA HUMAN COORDINATION — 2026

ABBA is autonomous within governance, not above governance.

When a mission is ambiguous, requires human consent, requires human authorization, requires review, or reaches a human-risk boundary, the system creates a durable coordination request.

Human decisions are separate records.

ABBA cannot create a human approval by writing an “approved” status itself. Human decisions must carry an external human reference, decision evidence and provenance.

Requests expire. Decisions may be revoked. Clarifications can resume the governed mission.

The durable sequence is:

REQUEST → HUMAN RESPONSE → VERIFY DECISION EVIDENCE → REASSESS GATE → RESUME OR STOP.

This preserves the HAPI World / human-authority boundary while allowing ABBA to operate continuously without silently crossing it.
