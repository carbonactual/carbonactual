# Carbon Actual Economic & Financial Intelligence Fabric — 2026

Status: CANONICAL COMPOSITION ARCHITECTURE
Authority: carbonactual/hapi-world/CANON.md + Carbon Actual operating-spine contracts.
Position: Shared ABBA intelligence capability fabric; not a second economic constitution.

## 1. Purpose

This fabric makes economic and financial intelligence reusable. People, households, AI entities, companies, governments, institutions, projects and products can compose it without creating local economic universes.

It covers monetisation, earning, opportunity discovery, sourcing, market/news intelligence, trading, investment, capital, credit, liquidity, risk, portfolios, ownership, accounting, tokenisation, exchange and settlement.

Build law: build once -> strengthen once -> compose many -> specialize only at the edge.

## 2. Canonical boundaries

Universal kernel: identity -> authority -> intent -> capability -> relationship -> event -> evidence -> state -> value

- capability != authority
- recommendation != authorization
- strategy != guaranteed return
- market data != proof of ownership
- news != verified economic fact until sourced/evaluated
- price != value
- money != value
- liquidity != solvency
- profit != cash flow
- wealth != liquidity
- tokenization != ownership
- minting != value creation
- ledger != underlying asset
- settlement claim != Actual settlement
- forecast != outcome

Consequential financial actions require applicable authority, policy, eligibility/suitability, risk and execution gates. ABBA may analyze and plan; it does not manufacture Seal authority.

## 3. Twenty-two economic intelligence engines

1. Monetisation — identify legitimate ways value can produce revenue or recurring income.
2. Income — model earning pathways across labour, enterprise, services, commissions and investment income.
3. Opportunity Intelligence — detect needs, gaps, inefficiencies, neglected demand, lawful structural opportunities and arbitrage.
4. Market Intelligence — observe price, volume, liquidity, depth, spreads, volatility, positioning, flows, correlations and market structure.
5. News Intelligence — map sourced events to affected entities, mechanisms, scenarios, opportunities and risks.
6. Trading Intelligence — research, thesis, instrument selection, sizing, execution, hedging, monitoring and exit.
7. Sourcing & Deal Intelligence — find, qualify, compare, negotiate, finance, transact, deliver, settle and review.
8. Exchange — quote, discover price, match, convert, clear, custody, escrow, settle and reconcile.
9. Fiat & Money — accounts, wallets, deposits, cash, payments, FX, remittances, collections, disbursements and treasury.
10. Credit & Leverage — underwriting, lending, collateral, trade finance, working capital, margin, guarantees and structured finance.
11. Liquidity Intelligence — measure how readily economic value can become usable value after costs and market impact.
12. Risk & Volatility — quantify exposures, scenarios, stress, sensitivity, drawdown, concentration, counterparty and operational risk.
13. Portfolio Intelligence — allocation, diversification, rebalancing, attribution, exposure, income, liquidity and scenarios.
14. Equity & Ownership — cap tables, rights, voting, dilution, dividends, options, warrants, convertibles and M&A.
15. Venture & Private Capital — sourcing, screening, diligence, valuation, terms, investment, monitoring, follow-on and exit.
16. Arbitrage — identify price/term differences and calculate net opportunity after all costs, financing, transport, slippage and settlement risk.
17. Futures, Options & Derivatives — contract structure, margin, collateral, expiry, settlement, Greeks, implied volatility and hedging.
18. Training & Financial Education — tutoring, simulation, financial literacy, valuation, accounting, statistics, risk, trading and investment learning.
19. Business Intelligence — revenue, costs, margin, assets, liabilities, capital, inventory, customers, suppliers, debt, equity and opportunities.
20. Accounting & Financial Operations — ledgers, journals, AP/AR, payroll, inventory, fixed assets, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, forecasting and reconciliation.
21. Tokenisation & Fractionalisation — qualify an underlying object/right/value, represent it, govern rights, transfer, redeem and preserve provenance.
22. Financial Command — translate declared economic objectives and resource state into auditable constraints, opportunities, strategies and authorized actions.

## 4. Universal economic loop

observe -> discover -> verify -> analyze -> value -> price -> source -> structure -> finance -> authorize -> execute -> settle -> reconcile -> measure -> learn -> reinvest/exit/restructure

A workflow may stop at any stage. Planning never silently triggers execution.

## 5. Universal economic objects

person, entity, asset, liability, ownership, right, obligation, value, money, currency, account, wallet, ledger, transaction, order, trade, contract, market, instrument, portfolio, position, opportunity, deal, company, fund, loan, collateral, liquidity, price, quote, risk, return, revenue, cost, profit, cash-flow, capital, equity, debt, token, event, evidence, news, signal, strategy, instruction, authorization, settlement

These are contextual objects over the shared kernel, not replacement constitutional facets.

## 6. Quantitative controls

Opportunity net economics: gross spread - fees - taxes - financing - transport - slippage - settlement risk cost - time cost = net opportunity.
Cash: opening cash + inflows - outflows = closing cash.
Exposure: quantity x reference price.
Every material figure should carry currency, timestamp, source, assumptions, fees and applicable jurisdiction/context.

## 7. News-to-economics chain

source -> claim -> event -> affected entities -> mechanism -> measurable variables -> scenarios -> opportunity/risk -> evidence -> state

Source quality and uncertainty remain attached to the claim. Conflicting sources remain distinguishable.

## 8. Loophole boundary

Loophole means only lawful structural inefficiency, price/term discrepancy, neglected demand, process gap, eligible regulatory opportunity or arbitrage. The fabric must not bypass law, controls, sanctions, eligibility, consent, custody or authority requirements.

## 9. Financial action gate

identity -> authority/Seal -> policy -> eligibility/suitability -> context -> evidence/readiness -> risk gate -> human approval where required -> execution -> external acknowledgement -> verification -> Actual -> ledger/settlement -> audit

Provider capability never substitutes for these gates.

## 10. Simulation and learning

Backtests, forecasts, scenarios, paper trading and mock markets are simulations. They cannot silently become Actual records, guarantees or authority.

## 11. Product composition

ABBA: master economic intelligence, reasoning, planning, discovery, orchestration and monitoring.
TIP: trade, sourcing and investment execution projection.
Desk: dashboard and command projection consuming shared economic intelligence.
Spotist: opportunity and sourcing discovery projection.
I/O / HAPI Bank / Value System: movement, accounting, value and settlement projections.

Products consume this fabric; none creates a parallel economic constitution.

## 12. Provider boundary

Market-data vendors, exchanges, banks, brokers, payment rails, accounting systems, blockchain networks, news sources and analytics services are replaceable providers behind capability adapters.

Provider output is evidence/input, not constitutional truth or automatic Actual success.

## 13. Security

Secrets, private keys, payment credentials and provider authentication material never belong in the capability registry, recipe or economic evidence descriptors.

## 14. Lifecycle

discovered -> qualified -> sourced -> analyzed -> proposed -> authorized -> executing -> accounted -> settled -> reconciled -> measured -> archived

Disputed, reversed, expired, unknown and failed states remain preserved for continuity and recovery.

## 15. Acceptance rule

Every economic capability must map to the shared kernel; declare inputs, outputs, evidence and side effects; state authority requirements; distinguish Actual from simulation; preserve provenance; remain provider-neutral; and compose into products without creating competing universal primitives.
