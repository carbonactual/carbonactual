# Carbon Actual Canonical Graph Model

**Status: CANONICAL CONTRACT**

The Carbon Actual ecosystem is modeled as one governed graph.

`Node --[typed Relationship]--> Node`

## Nodes

A node may represent a human, AI, organization, institution, government, contract, resource, asset, product, service, community, territory, agent, workflow, or other registered object.

## Edges

Relationships are first-class objects/edges with, where applicable:

- source and target
- relationship type
- authority context
- provenance/evidence
- lifecycle/status
- temporal validity
- constraints

Dependencies are semantically distinct from ordinary relationships.

## Events

Events record changes, observations and consequential occurrences around graph objects. They preserve attribution, provenance, time, correlation and outcome where applicable.

## Graph rules

- No product may create a second universal graph.
- Domain graphs are views/compositions of the canonical graph.
- Relationship semantics are registered once and reused.
- Historical relationships remain available where required for provenance.
- Graph writes pass through identity, authority and policy controls.

## One-spine principle

The graph is the common connective tissue of the Carbon Actual ecosystem. Products and future compositions are graph participants, not isolated graph systems.
