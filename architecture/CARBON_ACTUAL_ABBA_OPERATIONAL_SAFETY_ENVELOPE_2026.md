# CARBON ACTUAL — ABBA OPERATIONAL SAFETY ENVELOPE — 2026

Before consequential execution, ABBA evaluates a technical safety envelope independently of authority.

The envelope bounds cost, concurrency, retries, blast radius, data egress, reversibility and provider failure conditions.

Circuit breakers may hold or block execution. They never become authority.

The execution order is:

TECHNICAL SUBSTRATE READINESS → OPERATIONAL SAFETY → AUTHORITY/POLICY/CONSENT → EXECUTION.

A safety assessment is not an authorization. A budget ceiling is not permission. A provider route is not consent.

Safety controls are fail-closed and resumable through governed continuation.
