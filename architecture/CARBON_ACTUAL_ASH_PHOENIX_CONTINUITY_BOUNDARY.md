# Carbon Actual Ash / Phoenix / Continuity Boundary

Status: canonical technical contract.

## Ash

Ash is the retained classification space for residual, failed, unknown, unreconciled or security-relevant output that should not be silently discarded.

```text
residual / anomaly
  -> classify
  -> ASH
  -> recover / reuse / repair / repurpose / recycle
                 OR
             security response
```

Ash is not a trash/deletion mechanism and is not an alternative ledger or authority system.

## Phoenix

Phoenix is the governed response mechanism for materially harmful, fraudulent, unauthorized or security-nonconforming conditions.

Possible response actions include isolation, quarantine, revoke, block, rate-limit, trace, notify, restore and recovery subject to policy.

Phoenix does not become general-purpose orchestration or economic authority.

## Continuity

Critical resources, identity, evidence, configuration, provider relationships and product state should define:

```text
backup
recovery
rotation
migration
revocation
replacement
replay/rebuild
exit/mobility
```

A compromised integration must be compartmentalized so that the failure does not imply ecosystem-wide authority or data compromise.

## Unknown

Unknown state is retained when classification is incomplete. The system must not infer trust, ownership, identity or authority solely from an unknown input.

## Human safety boundary

Where a domain requires specialized professional, legal, financial or medical controls, the relevant branch must introduce its required regulated workflow without promoting the regulation into a competing universal primitive.
