# SPOTIST — Canonical Seek / Discovery Capability

**Status: CANONICAL ECOSYSTEM CAPABILITY**
**Domain:** Discovery, seeking, matching, locating, tracing and opportunity discovery
**Authority:** Carbon Actual operating-spine contracts plus HAPI World constitutional authority, privacy, safety, evidence and consent rules

## Purpose

SPOTIST is the ecosystem-wide capability for seeking: discovering, locating, identifying, matching, tracing, qualifying, verifying, connecting, assembling or monitoring whatever is legitimately being sought.

SPOTIST is not limited to products, services, procurement, suppliers, markets or commerce. The list of legitimate seek targets is open-world and non-exhaustive.

## Canonical abstraction

```text
SEEK
  ↓
INTERPRET INTENT
  ↓
DEFINE / DECOMPOSE CRITERIA
  ↓
DISCOVER
  ↓
MATCH / TRACE / LOCATE
  ↓
QUALIFY
  ↓
VERIFY / EVIDENCE
  ↓
CONNECT / ALERT / ASSEMBLE
  ↓
HAND OFF TO THE APPROPRIATE CAPABILITY
  ↓
OPTIONALLY ACT UNDER AUTHORITY
```

SPOTIST is therefore a capability domain, not a fixed application category.

## Natural-language first

The human should not need to know the internal category before seeking. The input may be precise, vague, incomplete, unusual, compound or exploratory. SPOTIST adapts the seek model to expressed intent rather than forcing a predetermined category.

## Tailored seeking

Authorized context may include explicit criteria, preferences, constraints, budget, geography, timing, saved/rejected seeks, relationships, accessibility requirements, permissions, historical outcomes and standing requests. Personalization remains transparent and bounded by privacy and authority rules.

## Discovery is not certainty

SPOTIST MUST preserve distinctions between exact match, strong match, possible match, substitute, lead, unverified lead, evidence-backed finding, conflicting information, historical information, inferred relationship and unresolved seek.

A discovery result MUST NOT be represented as established fact merely because a search produced it. Evidence, provenance, confidence, contradiction and verification status remain explicit.

## Compound seeks and missions

A seek may contain multiple dependencies. SPOTIST may decompose a mission into related seeks, discover compatible components, assemble candidates and hand the resulting chain to ABBA or another authorized orchestrator. SPOTIST does not thereby become the authority for consequential action.

## Serendipity and opportunity discovery

SPOTIST may discover materially relevant substitutes, scarce items, exceptional offers, newly available capacity, people who may solve a problem, or related opportunities. Such discoveries are distinguished from direct matches and should explain the relevance basis.

## Two-way seeking

SPOTIST supports both:

- `I am looking for X`;
- `I have X / can do X / know X / can provide X`.

Matching may connect seekers, holders, providers, capabilities, opportunities and relationships.

## Monitoring / radar

A seek may remain active. Authorized sources can be monitored for new matches, changing conditions, expiring offers, relevant events, newly available people/capabilities, new evidence or opportunities. Standing seeks retain scope, authority, privacy, expiry and notification rules.

## People and sensitive discovery

People may be sought by legitimate role, capability, availability, qualification, language, experience, representation or geography. Sensitive people-related seeking is subject to identity, consent, privacy, safeguarding, legal and authority constraints. SPOTIST must not become unrestricted surveillance, stalking, doxxing or unauthorized disclosure infrastructure.

## Lost, missing and recovery seeks

SPOTIST may support lawful discovery and recovery of lost property, pets, documents, records, missing connections and provenance leads, preserving evidence and uncertainty rather than presenting speculation as confirmed identification.

## Opportunity seeks

SPOTIST may search for offers, promotions, bargains, surplus/underused resources, contracts, grants, partnerships, available capacity and other opportunities. It discovers and matches; the relevant economic domain governs transaction and value movement.

## Adventures, experiences and actions

A seek may target an experience rather than a pre-existing product. SPOTIST may discover or compose adventures, outings, educational/cultural experiences, events, group activities and constraint-driven experiences, then hand off execution to the appropriate event, economic, communication or orchestration capability.

## Product boundary

SPOTIST may be exposed as a standalone product, product module, dashboard capability, API/service, agent capability, internal service, workflow or embedded seek surface. It must not require a dedicated product shell merely to exist.

## Composition boundaries

```text
Carbon Actual → operating-spine contracts and constitutional interoperability
ABBA          → intelligence, reasoning and orchestration
SPOTIST       → seeking, discovery, matching, tracing and finding
DESK          → participant workspace/dashboard that may expose SPOTIST
OMNI          → separate current product surface
I/O           → value circulation, ledger and settlement infrastructure
HAPI          → human identity/authority/continuity interfaces
```

A product may consume SPOTIST without becoming SPOTIST or duplicating its semantics.

## Existing sourcing capability remains valid

Sourcing, procurement, supplier discovery, manufacturing discovery, demand aggregation, capacity discovery, quotation, qualification, negotiation and trade-related discovery are valid SPOTIST applications, but do not define the whole capability.

## Relationship to ABBA

ABBA may interpret a request, decompose a mission, invoke SPOTIST, compare results, coordinate follow-on capabilities, monitor an active seek, and ask for human clarification or approval. SPOTIST supplies discovery capability; ABBA remains the ecosystem orchestration intelligence.

## Authority, safety and privacy

SPOTIST discovery does not itself grant authority. Sensitive information, financial commitments, regulated services, physical intervention, private records, binding agreements, value movement and consequential communications require the relevant authority, consent, policy, safeguarding, evidence and governance mechanisms.

## No forced taxonomy

SPOTIST remains open-world. A new seek type does not require a constitutional rewrite merely because the ecosystem has not encountered it. Unknown/provisional objects may be progressively classified through evidence and governed extension.

## Canonical invariant

> **SPOTIST is the ecosystem capability for finding what is legitimately being sought — whatever that may be — and for turning a natural-language seek into discoverable, evidence-aware, qualified and appropriately hand-offable results.**
