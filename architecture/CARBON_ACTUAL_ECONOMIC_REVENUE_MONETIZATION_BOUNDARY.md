# Carbon Actual Economic Revenue & Monetization Boundary

**Status:** CANONICAL CROSS-CUTTING ECONOMIC CONTRACT  
**Authority:** HAPI World `CANON.md` + Carbon Actual economic/value contracts

## Purpose

Carbon Actual may capture economic value from legitimate products, services, infrastructure, institutions and exchanges without turning monetization into a hidden authority, ownership or user-manipulation system.

Revenue is an economic outcome. It is not a constitutional right.

## Separation

`Value ≠ Price ≠ Revenue ≠ Profit ≠ Cashflow ≠ Settlement ≠ Authority`

A product may:
- create value;
- charge a fee;
- receive revenue;
- incur costs;
- create liabilities;
- share revenue;
- facilitate settlement;
- record economics.

Those are separate states and claims.

## Revenue object

A monetization record should be able to represent:
- payer;
- beneficiary;
- service/product/capability;
- pricing basis;
- quantity/unit;
- currency or value unit;
- tax/levy treatment where applicable;
- discount/subsidy/sponsorship;
- platform fee;
- provider fee;
- commission;
- revenue share;
- refund/reversal;
- timing;
- settlement method;
- authority/consent context;
- evidence/provenance;
- jurisdiction;
- obligations/liabilities.

No revenue record should silently imply ownership of the underlying customer, asset, capability or relationship.

## Permitted economic models

The ecosystem may compose:
- subscription;
- usage-based pricing;
- fixed service fee;
- transaction fee;
- marketplace fee;
- brokerage/commission;
- implementation/integration fee;
- support/maintenance fee;
- licensing;
- royalty;
- membership;
- sponsorship;
- advertising where lawful and transparently disclosed;
- financing/interest/profit-share structures where lawful;
- revenue sharing;
- cooperative/community economic models;
- public or institutional contract revenue;
- grants or subsidies.

Products choose the models relevant to their domain. The canonical system defines their accounting/evidence boundaries, not a single commercial model.

## Full-cost and liability rule

Revenue cannot be evaluated without relevant costs and obligations.

Where appropriate, the economic record should preserve:
- monetary cost;
- time cost;
- energy;
- transport;
- water;
- waste;
- emissions;
- support burden;
- infrastructure consumption;
- fraud/default risk;
- regulatory obligations;
- refunds/chargebacks;
- future maintenance;
- environmental or social externalities where measurable.

Pulse and Value remain distinct. A positive revenue result does not automatically mean positive ecosystem Value.

## Consent and transparency

Monetization must disclose, where applicable:
- what is being charged;
- why;
- by whom;
- amount/unit;
- frequency;
- taxes/fees;
- cancellation/refund terms;
- material dependencies;
- relevant data use;
- sponsorship/advertising relationships.

Consent must be explicit where required. Dark patterns, hidden fees and deceptive default escalation are prohibited by the applicable product/security/consumer-protection boundary.

## Agentic commerce

An agent may negotiate or execute monetized actions only within:
- principal identity;
- delegated authority;
- permitted product/object class;
- spending or pricing limits;
- counterparty constraints;
- jurisdiction;
- policy;
- approval threshold.

An agent cannot create a new revenue entitlement merely because a monetization mechanism is technically available.

## Accounting boundary

Revenue capture must integrate with:
- Value System for value classification;
- I/O for movement and settlement;
- canonical ledgers for accounting/provenance;
- evidence and event contracts;
- jurisdiction-specific financial/tax/compliance adapters.

No product should create a second economic ledger merely to calculate its own revenue.

## Refunds, reversals and disputes

Corrections must preserve history.

`charge → settlement → refund/reversal/adjustment → evidence → reconciliation`

A correction must not erase the original event.

## Non-exploitation rule

The ecosystem should capture value from useful outcomes, not from deliberate confusion, unnecessary lock-in, hidden dependency or suppression of user exit.

Portability and exit remain economic capabilities.

## Invariants

1. Revenue is not authority.
2. Price is not value.
3. Transaction fee is not ownership.
4. Payment is not settlement until the applicable settlement evidence exists.
5. Revenue does not erase liability/cost.
6. Monetization cannot bypass consent or policy.
7. Agent capability does not create spending authority.
8. No product creates a competing universal economic ledger.
9. Historical economic events remain reconstructable.
10. Legitimate exit and portability are not monetization failures to be obstructed.
